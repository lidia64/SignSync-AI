Making healthcare accessible for ALL

A multimodal AI interpreter that translates continuous African Sign Language into spoken Kinyarwanda and English in real-time, empowering the Deaf community in medical settings.

Launch Live Interpreter Prototype

⚠️ The Problem

Millions of deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals across Africa face severe, life-threatening communication barriers in healthcare.

When a patient cannot articulate their symptoms, and clinicians lack access to human sign language interpreters, misdiagnoses occur. Current accessible technologies are often built only for western languages or require high-end hardware.

💡 Our GenAI Solution

SignBridge AI eliminates this barrier using state-of-the-art multimodal Generative AI. We don't just track static hands—we read continuous motion.

  • Video Context Analyzes 4-second video clips to understand full sentence structure, not just isolated words.
  • Gemini 3 Powered Utilizes the Gemini 3 Flash Preview model for lightning-fast spatial and temporal reasoning.
  • Localized Output Natively translates medical signs into Kinyarwanda text and speech.
